About the Movie

Iron Man (2008) is a superhero film based on Marvel Comics. It follows Tony Stark, a billionaire genius who builds a powerful suit of armor to escape captivity and becomes the superhero Iron Man.

Story

Tony Stark, a weapons manufacturer, is captured by terrorists. Instead of building weapons, he secretly creates a high-tech armored suit to escape. Later, he upgrades the suit and decides to use it to protect the world.

Main Cast

  • Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark / Iron Man
  • Gwyneth Paltrow as Pepper Potts
  • Jeff Bridges as Obadiah Stane
  • Terrence Howard as James Rhodes
